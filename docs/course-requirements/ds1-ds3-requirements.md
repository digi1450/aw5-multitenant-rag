# Official Deliverable Requirements (DS1–DS3)

The text below is preserved from the instructor's announcement supplied by the project owner.

## Deliverable Set 1 (DS1)

Deliverable Set 1 (DS1) contains only the Project Concept which accounts for 10% of your final grade.

- Only one member of your group needs to submit a single pdf file, which must be named: `TOPICID-ProjectConcept.pdf`
- For example, if your TOPICID is PA1, then the file name should be `PA1-ProjectConcept.pdf`.
- The deadline of this submission is 31 August.
- The Project Concept will be included again in Deliverable Set 2 (DS2), which includes also some Requirements Specification and Mockup of your proposed system/framework. DS2 will be submitted before an onsite presentation during (Sep 28 to Oct 2 - the week after mid exams). Please see the post about DS2 for details.

## Deliverable Set 2 (DS2)

DS2 should contains three parts:

1. Project Concept which you have submitted in DS1. However you can make any revisions.
2. Requirements Specification
3. Any kind of Mockup, examples, demonstration etc of your proposed system or framework.

Together three parts account for 25% of your final grade (the Project concept is 10%, and the other two parts account for 15%).

- Only one member of your group need to submit a single file, which must be named: `TOPICID-RequirementSpec.pdf`
- The deadline for submission DS2 is Sept 28.
- After this submission, each group needs to give a presentation onsite. There will be 3 sessions. You will be able to select one of three sessions. In each session, there will be a committee consisting of three Professors to evaluate your DS2.

## Deliverable Set 3 (DS3)

DS3 should contain all the parts of DS2, and additionally some Design Specification and Prototype of your proposed system or concrete results of your study.

- The score of DS3 will be account for 55% of your final score.
- The deadline for submitting DS3 is TBA. Your group needs to submit just one pdf file, which must be named `TOPICID-DesignSpec.pdf`.
- After the submission of DS3, we will have final presentations during (TBA). There will be three sessions. Each session is administered by a committee consisting of three professors. You can select one of these three sessions to present.
